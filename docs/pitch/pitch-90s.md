# MAPAY: 90-second pitch

**Target spoken length:** about 220 words. Screen directions are not spoken.

## 1. The commute — hook

**On screen:** Map centered on Miami; show the BBC → MMC route and the congestion, flood-risk and construction layers.

Leaving BBC at 5:10, you hit red on the Palmetto. On the way home, the group chat says your street is underwater. That’s Miami: same commute, new surprise. MAPAY knows your routine and the street conditions that can change it.

## 2. The heads-up

**On screen:** Show the FIU routine, tap **Fire heads-up now**, then show the notification or widget on the Lock Screen.

Our FIU routine: MMC to BBC at 9:30, back between five and seven. The heads-up says when to leave and what’s ahead. Start opens Google Maps; Customize changes the route.

## 3. Customize the route

**On screen:** Open Customize, enter the prompt, then show the old and new routes.

“Stop at a Starbucks and stay off the Palmetto.” Gemini reads the prompt; MAPAY’s deterministic router picks the road. Coffee is a real stop. If Google misses a flood or closure, MAPAY adds a visible, removable steering stop: “this keeps you off flooded NE 151st Street.” Customize sends both in one Google Maps link.

## 4. The map’s data

**On screen:** Return to the map; tap a flood or construction feature to show its source and timestamp. Use a verified satellite pass if one is available.

Now the map explains why. Cyan marks predicted flood risk from tides and flood zones; orange marks city construction. Tap for sources and timestamps; satellite evidence appears when a pass covers Miami.

## 5. Close

**On screen:** Show the customized route and **Open in Google Maps**.

MAPAY doesn’t replace Google Maps; it steers it. Those extra stops? That’s us routing you around the flood on 151st before Google knows it’s there. Preview the route in MAPAY, then hand off for the drive: live traffic, voice, lane guidance, CarPlay and rerouting you trust. No new app to learn while driving. Fewer surprises between campus and home—that’s the idea.
